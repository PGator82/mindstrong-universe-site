Why This Matters

An expression is math compressed into symbols. Once you can translate it into words, variables stop feeling mysterious.

Key Concept
Expressions describe quantities and operations without an equals sign. Variables stand for values, and structure tells you what is being added, multiplied, grouped, or compared.

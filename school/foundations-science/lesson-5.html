Adaptations & Survival

Nature's Solutions to Life's Challenges

Mode:

The camel's hump, the cactus's spines, the arctic fox's white coat — every feature is millions of years of problem-solving. Evolution doesn't design; it selects. And the results are extraordinary.

📚 Core Concepts

🦴
Structural Adaptations
Physical features that help an organism survive: thick fur, sharp claws, long beaks, waterproof feathers, hollow bones, camouflage coloring. These are inherited body traits.
🔄
Behavioral Adaptations
Actions that help survival: hibernation (sleeping through winter), migration (moving to better conditions), nocturnal behavior (active at night), and camouflage behavior.
🧬
Natural Selection
Organisms with traits better suited to their environment survive and reproduce more. Over generations, those beneficial traits become more common in the population — this is evolution in action.
